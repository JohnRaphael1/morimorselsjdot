THE STORY

ABOUT

MoriMorsels JDot is an emerging drill rapper and songwriter carving his own lane with raw energy, storytelling, and unapologetic authenticity.

Born John Raphael Chukwuebuka, he's been building his catalog on SoundCloud since 2024 with tracks like "10 Times." In 2025 he leveled up with powerful singles like "Don't Play With Me," solidifying his sound and gaining recognition as a rising voice in drill and hip-hop.

With influences from Juice WRLD, XXXTENTACION, Polo G, King Von, Lil Durk, YNW Melly, Rema, Davido, Wizkid, 21 Savage, Tupac, Chief Keef, Drake, Hurricane Wisdom, Playboi Carti, Future, Young Thug, Lil Uzi Vert, and Gunna — JDot delivers a global sound rooted in his own reality.

His music isn't just about rhythm — it's about ambition, survival, and making a name in the rap game.

Also Known As PureEvil · Apijal · Deucalion
Genres Trap · Hip-Hop/Rap · Emo Rap · Melodic Rap · Drill
